## Teaching

You think kindness has a size? Like a pot on the stove. Fill it, clap on the lid, you're a generous person.

A Jew is born kind. That is not the compliment you think it is. Born-kind has a lid. You give until it pinches, then you say, "That's me being good." The Tanya says your compassion has a measure. G-d's does not.

So after the Temple is gone and we are in exile, the prophet says a line people mishear. They think he is telling us G-d's kindnesses never ran out. The Alter Rebbe hears another sentence: we are not complete. That is why we have to give the way G-d gives. No lid.

A husband says, "I already did the dishes twice this week."
She is not asking for a quota. She is asking if this is a home.

G-d is not collecting your tenth while the world is still broken. He wants you to behave like Him down here, so His unlimited compassion has a place to land. Israel is not redeemed by the kindness we owed. We are redeemed by the kindness nobody could have demanded.

## What to do

Find the lid on this week's kindness and take it off once.

Give something that was not on the list.

## Takeaway

Give the way G-d gives, because we are not finished yet.

## Remember

We learn this so something moves. Not so we collected a nice idea.
