## Teaching

There's giving that is just you being you. And there's giving that is work.

A Jewish soul comes out of G-d's own kindness. Kindness is stronger in us than the holding-back. That is why a Jew hears a sad story and the hand is already in the pocket. The Tanya calls that the "act" of tzedakah. Act, like something that happens by itself. Like breathing.

Then there is service. Service is when you do what your nature would not do. You cancel your taste, your budget, your "that's enough," because He wants something else.

"I already gave."
"Good. That was you. Now do the part that isn't you."

The Torah says give, you shall give, even a hundred times. Not because G-d likes money. Because He wants a human being who can put his own heart on the side and still open the door. The easy kindness is beautiful. The kindness that costs your nature is what He came down here for.

## What to do

Give once from nature. Then give once against it.

## Takeaway

The gift that is already you is an act. The gift that isn't you is service.

## Remember

We learn this so something moves. Not so we collected a nice idea.
