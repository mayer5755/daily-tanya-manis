## Teaching

What's the first job of a believer? Not to fix your mood. To live.

The Tanya says: first of all, be glad. Every hour. Actually live by the fact that G-d is giving you life this second, and He is being good to you this second. Fact. Not feeling.

A woman stands at the washing machine. The sock is missing again. She sighs like the universe picked her. That is not a sock problem. That is a statement: something is a little evil, I am a little short on good. The people who knew G-d from the inside pushed that statement out of the house.

Yes and no in worldly stuff are supposed to weigh the same. If they don't, you are not serving G-d. You are serving you.

"So I should fake a smile?"
"No. Stop treating your life like a product review."

You were put here to be tested: do you want the life that comes from a living G-d, or the life that comes from your appetites? Believe the first one, and even the thing that looked bad gets swallowed into the good that was hiding in it. G-d wants that faith on earth, where it costs something.

## What to do

Catch one complaint today and ask if it is a verdict on G-d.

## Takeaway

Live the fact that He is good this second. Don't wait to feel it.

## Remember

We learn this so something moves. Not so we collected a nice idea.
