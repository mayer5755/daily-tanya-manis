## Teaching

Why would a gift of money make peace? Because the world is an argument.

Water wants to go down. Fire wants to go up. Kindness pours. Strength holds back. The Tanya says they don't put each other out. How? G-d shows up. Not as a compromise. As light that is not a size.

In the kitchen it looks like this. One spouse wants to give the kids whatever they ask. The other wants a rule. If they stay as two policies, the house is a war. If they remember Who the house is for, the giving and the holding become one thing. That is not a truce. That is a home.

The verse says the act of tzedakah will be peace. You give life to someone who has nothing of his own, and upstairs the argument relaxes.

G-d wants that down here. Not nicer people. A world where giving and holding stop canceling each other because He is in the room.

## What to do

Where "give" and "hold back" are two armies in your house, ask Who the house is for.

## Takeaway

Peace is not a truce between opposites. It is G-d in the room.

## Remember

We learn this so something moves. Not so we collected a nice idea.
