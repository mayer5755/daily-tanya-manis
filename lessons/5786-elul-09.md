## Teaching

Does G-d just decide to make peace? The Tanya says no. Upstairs waits for downstairs.

You want a revelation, the kind that makes opposing forces stop fighting. It starts when you give life, kindness, mercy to a person who has nothing of his own. You revive a low spirit.

One of the Sages put a coin in a poor man's hand, then he prayed. First the kitchen table. Then you talk to G-d.

This world is mixed. Good and not-good in the same pot. The whole world does not get sorted until the end. You? You get a taste every time you steal a few minutes alone with your Maker, if you came there from a real act.

G-d wants His face seen in this world. He told you how. Give first. Then look.

## What to do

Give someone life before you ask G-d for a conversation.

## Takeaway

Heaven answers the table you already set.

## Remember
