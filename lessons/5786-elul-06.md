## Teaching

You want a good life. Who doesn't? Kids. Health. A paycheck that shows up. The Tanya opens this letter with a slap: that is not the way G-d's light lives in a person.

Not because those things are dirty. Because if that is what you want, you are wanting a something. G-d is a Who. You were not created to collect a nicer something.

Picture yourself at the fridge at midnight. The light goes on. For one second you exist because He is making you exist. Not last year. This second. From nothing. If the One making you right now is only good, all day, how did you decide an empty shelf is a tragedy?

"But I need groceries."
"Need them. Don't worship them."

The Sages said you live against your will. Meaning: you did not sign up for a body. You signed up to believe there is no empty spot, and in the King's face there is life. That is the faith a human being was born for. G-d wants someone down here who can look at a hard Tuesday and still know Who is in the kitchen.

## What to do

When you catch yourself wanting a "normal life," name the Who, not the pile.

## Takeaway

Want the Who who is making you, not a better pile of what.

## Remember

We learn this so something moves. Not so we collected a nice idea.
