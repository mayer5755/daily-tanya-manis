## Teaching

Elul, so everyone’s in the mood to feel bad. The Alter Rebbe is not impressed.

He says the main part of teshuvah is the heart. Fine. You can sit there and mean it. You can cry. You can have a very spiritual afternoon.

Then he ruins it.

That feeling, he says, wakes something infinite. But infinite light does not move in because you had a moment. It moves in when you do something that has no lid on it. Kindness to a person who has nothing. Not the neat ten percent. Not “I already gave.” The kind of giving where you stop counting, because the other person cannot pay you back.

Measured giving gets you measured kindness from Above. Same size box, both ways. If your soul is still broken, you would not cheap out on a doctor. So why is the soul cheaper?

The twenty percent cap? That’s for someone who’s already whole. If you’re in Elul, you’re not whole. You’re in the shop.

So don’t tell me about your regret. Tell me who ate because you meant it.

That’s today’s Tanya. Return is a heart. And a heart that never spends is just a thought you had.

## One line

Teshuvah that stays in the heart, and never spends, is still only a thought.

## Talk about it

- Where is your teshuvah still only in the head?
- What would kindness with no lid look like this week, without being reckless?
- When is the 20% cap wisdom, and when is it an excuse?
