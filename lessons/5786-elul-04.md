## Teaching (WhatsApp voice)

You want to do teshuvah. So you sit there and feel terrible. Very sincere. Very spiritual.

The Alter Rebbe is not against the feeling. He says that’s the main part. Teshuvah lives in the heart. Deep regret wakes a deep light. Not a little kindness from Above. Rav chesed. Kindness with the lid off. The kind that can actually fix what you broke.

Then he ruins the mood.

That light does not move in because you had a moment. A heart has to spend. Someone who has nothing of his own is standing in front of you. Give past the usual. Not the neat tenth. Not “I already gave.” Because before G-d, you also have nothing of your own. Same story, different wallet.

Measured giving, measured kindness. Same size box, both ways. Offerings in the Temple had a size. That’s why tzedakah can reach a repair they couldn’t. No shiur on a soul that needs healing.

And that famous one-fifth cap? That’s ordinary giving. If you’re trying to save a life, you don’t argue with the bill. The soul is not cheaper than the body.

So don’t tell me about your regret. Tell me who ate because you meant it.

## What to do

Let Elul start in the heart. Then make it leave the house.

Where you fell, add extra tzedakah. Not instead of regret. Because of it.

Find someone who actually lacks. Give past comfort.

Ask a rav before you empty the account. Generous is not reckless.

## Takeaway

Teshuvah begins in the heart. It is not finished there. When you go past your usual kindness, you wake G-d’s rav chesed, the kindness that can repair what the regular stuff could not.

## Remember

We learn this so something moves. Not so we collected a nice idea.
