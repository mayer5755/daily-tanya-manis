# Daily Tanya, in Manis Friedman's voice

Open-source daily Tanya teachings written in the public speaking style of Rabbi Manis Friedman. Original commentary. Links out to the sefer. Not a reprint of Kehot, Lessons in Tanya, or Chabad.org.

Manis asked for this to be open, so more people can learn Tanya, and so a teaching bot can sit on top of it.

## Today

[4 Elul 5786 / 17 Aug 2026](lessons/5786-elul-04.md) — Iggeret HaKodesh, Epistle 10, part 4.

## What this is

- One short teaching per Hebrew day, in his public English
- A calendar that knows regular year vs leap year (פ / מ)
- A voice guide so a bot can keep talking like him
- Links to Chabad.org / Sefaria for the actual Tanya

## What this is not

- Not his recorded voice, and not a clone
- Not Kehot's English, not Lessons in Tanya, not a scrape of Chabad.org
- The Alter Rebbe's Hebrew is public domain. Modern translations are not. We write our own teaching and point you at the book.

## Cycle, short

The Frierdiker Rebbe split Tanya into one lesson per Hebrew date, 19 Kislev to 18 Kislev. Leap years use a slower split. 5786 is a regular year. There is one official text per Hebrew day, not a morning and evening text.

Hayom Yom's Tanya lines follow 5703, a leap year. Do not copy those onto a regular year without checking.
